This unit is all about GenAI. This lecture will be about writing better prompts.

A prompt is a natural language text that requests the generative AI to perform a specific task.

If you give an AI a prompt such as "I want to eat somewhere fancy that serves Chinese food" it'll respond back with a list of places based on that prompt.

The AI is wrong sometimes and will just give you garbage knowledge.

A way to start a prompt is by doing a Task first, then the Persona(Who the AI is). and the Format: Output type.
Which is then followed by a Context. 
A good context would be "Give me some good ideas for a birthday present. Under $30."
A great context would be "Give me 5 ideas for a birthday present. My budget is $30. The gift is for a 29-yo who loves winter sports. And has recently switched to snowboarding from skiing."
Reference is additional input data, ideas. 
You then evaluate the output. 
Then the final step is to iterate.
Task -> Context -> Reference -> Evaluate -> Iterate -> Task.
